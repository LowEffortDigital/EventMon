# EventMon v2.3.0 (Build 23) — Release Notes

## Highlights
- **Tabbed Widget Component Designer (`Main Items`, `Header`, `Footer`)**: Customize the main event telemetry strip, header strip, and footer strip independently, with 1-click inclusion of items from `Main Items` into `Header` or `Footer`.
- **Header & Footer Chrome Toggles + Hover Move-Cross Mini-Toolbar**: Hide the header and/or footer chrome for ultra-compact (`44pt` min height) edge-to-edge full-card bleed. Hovering over a chromeless card reveals a floating mini-toolbar pill with a 4-way move-cross drag anchor, `+ Header` / `+ Footer` restore buttons, and close (`×`).
- **Environment Variables, Date/Time, Last Refresh & Static Text Tokens**: Add `$USER`, `$LOGNAME`, `$HOST`, `$HOME`, `$SHELL`, `$PATH`, `$TMPDIR`, `$LANG`, formatted Date/Time stamps, and relative/timestamped Last Refresh Time tokens to any widget zone or the macOS Menu Bar.
- **10 Font Families & Per-Token Typography Inspector**: Choose from 10 font families (`System Sans`, `Monospaced`, `Rounded`, `Serif`, `Avenir Next`, `Futura`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Menlo`, `Georgia`, `Chalkboard SE`), customize card Title/Readout/Footer font sizes, and inspect individual tokens for font family, size, weight, italic, underline, custom color, card visibility (`Hide in Card`), and overflow growth.
- **Custom Widget Surface Backgrounds & Interactive Corner Resize Grip**: Style individual widgets with `Theme Default`, `Solid Color`, `Two-Tone Gradient`, or `Procedural Wallpaper` (`Neon Synth Grid`, `Aurora Mesh`, `Carbon Matrix`, `Circuit Pulse`, `Deep Space`) and resize directly from the bottom-right corner grip.
- **Scrollbar-Free Full-Card Item Expansion**: Cards support up to 100 items and expand cleanly to fill the card area without ever displaying a scrollbar.
- **30-Step Undo & Copy Widget**: Undo recent widget edits with a single click, or duplicate any widget via `Copy…` with a new-name prompt.
